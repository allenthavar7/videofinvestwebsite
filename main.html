// The Council

Financial literacy, built by students, for students.

FINVEST sits within the FRCRCE student council system as the dedicated voice for finance, investing, and career readiness in commerce and capital markets.

Most engineering campuses teach you to build things. FINVEST exists to teach you what to do with what you build — how it's valued, funded, and grown. We're the council that brings markets, portfolios, and boardroom thinking onto a campus built for circuits and code.

From flagship case-study competitions to closed-room sessions with founders and analysts, every initiative is designed to be useful on day one — not theoretical, not distant. We want every member walking out with a sharper read on how capital actually moves.

// What we run

Four ways we put finance in front of the campus.

Each initiative is a different entry point — compete, listen, fund, or follow.

CASE COMP

Competitions

Inter-college case-study and stock-pitch competitions that put real portfolios and real deadlines in front of participants — judged by people who work in the field.

SPEAKER SERIES

Guest Sessions

Founders, analysts, and alumni in finance roles, brought in for closed-room conversations that go past the LinkedIn-post version of their career.

PARTNERSHIPS

Sponsors

Building relationships with firms and platforms willing to back student finance education — funding events, prizes, and resources for members.

MARKET WATCH

Content & Instagram

Bite-sized market commentary, event recaps, and campus finance news — keeping the council visible and useful between events, not just during them.
